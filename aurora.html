Three steps

Detailing without
the drama.

Keep the treatment small, controlled and consistent. The goal is a better-looking surface without turning your afternoon into a detailing project.

01 / PREP

Wash & dry

Remove dirt, dust and loose contamination. Work on a cool surface out of direct heat.

02 / TREAT

Apply lightly

Use a small amount on the affected area and work it evenly with a clean applicator or microfiber.

03 / FINISH

Buff to shine

Allow the product to haze as directed, then buff with a clean microfiber until the finish looks clear and even.

Know your surface

Good for light marks.
Not a magic wand.

Cosmetic surface treatments have limits, especially when the paint system itself is damaged.

Best candidates

Light surface scratches and swirls
Fine wash marks and haze
Minor dullness and oxidation appearance
Gloss refresh between details
Painted metal and suitable clear-coated panels

Call a professional when…

You can see bare metal or primer
The scratch is deep through the paint system
There is peeling, cracking or major clear-coat failure
The panel needs bodywork or repainting
You are unsure whether a surface is safe to treat
