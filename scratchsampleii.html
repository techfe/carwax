The real problem

Your car can look tired without needing a full respray.

Daily washing, dust, branches, parking-lot contact and fine swirl marks slowly flatten the gloss. The first move should be a smart cosmetic treatment—not automatically a body-shop bill.

3-step application

No complicated detailing setup.

STEP 01

Wash & dry

Remove dust and grit first. Never rub a dirty panel aggressively.

STEP 02

Apply lightly

Buff & inspect

FAQ

Remove doubts before they become exits.

Will it repair a deep scratch?

No cosmetic wax should be presented as a universal repair for deep gouges. If paint is missing or primer/metal is exposed, use a professional repair process.

Can I use it on any paint colour?

Test on a small inconspicuous area first and follow the product's real manufacturer instructions for compatible surfaces.

Do I need a machine polisher?

This page is designed around hand application for convenience. A machine can change the correction result and should be used with the right pad, compound and technique.

How often should I use it?

Use the manufacturer's actual maintenance guidance. Avoid inventing a fixed protection period when the product's technical data has not been supplied.
