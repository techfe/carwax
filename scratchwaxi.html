FAQ

Before you order.

Will it remove a deep scratch?

Do not assume so. If the scratch has cut through the paint layer, a wax/polish treatment cannot replace missing paint. The safest approach is to test a small area and seek professional repair for deep damage.

Does it work on every colour?

The source page markets it for multiple colours and paint types. Actual results depend on the surface condition, finish and product compatibility, so spot-testing is sensible.
